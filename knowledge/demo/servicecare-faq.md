# Demonstration ServiceCare FAQ

NOT an approved BMW manual. Generic demonstration content only. Do not ingest this into a production Knowledge Base. Production instructions must be reviewed for the vehicle model, software version and market.

## Reconnecting a phone

When safely parked, use the mobile-device menu and the phone's Bluetooth settings. Follow the pairing instructions in the model-specific owner's manual and confirm matching pairing codes. Menu names vary. If unsuccessful, ask an advisor and include the phone model and vehicle software version.

## Service intervals

Use the model-specific service requirements display and owner's manual. Service intervals vary with model, use and market. An advisor should confirm the next required appointment.

## Warranty

Coverage depends on vehicle, market, mileage and service history. Check the warranty booklet and ask an advisor to verify eligibility before authorizing a repair.

## Safety exclusions

Brake, steering, smoke, fire, overheating, accident damage, battery safety and critical-warning concerns require human escalation, not a self-service resolution. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services or roadside assistance. The portal is not an emergency service.
