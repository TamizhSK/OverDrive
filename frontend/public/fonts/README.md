# Supplied BMW Type Next Fonts

`bmw-type-next-regular.otf` and `bmw-type-next-bold.otf` are the unmodified user-supplied font binaries. Their OpenType headers identify CFF fonts; their embedded OS/2 weights are 400 and 700. Original filenames were `BMWTypeNext Pro Regular.ttf` and `BMWTypeNext Pro.ttf`.

They are self-hosted, not loaded from a third-party font service. BMW Type Next is the property of BMW AG and is used here for a non-commercial demonstration only. No font licence or redistribution rights are granted by this repository.
